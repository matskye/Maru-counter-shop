How to play

  • Shifts are the main way to learn: a few minutes of customers, mixing reviews that are due with a little new material. Maru introduces counters one at a time and brings back what you miss.
  • Serve: a customer orders with a number and a counter, like 「3本ください」. Put that many matching items on the counter (click, tap or drag), then press Done.
  • Order: the customer names the item and says how many with a native number, like 「りんごをみっつください」. Put exactly that on the counter.
  • Count: items are on the counter. Pick (or type) the right way to say how many there are. Watch for sound changes such as さんぼん and いっぽん.
  • Yen come from answering right first time in a shift, with a bonus for answers in a row and for a perfect shift. Takings grow the shop's rank and buy decorations; the stamp card marks your milestones.
  • Story: each chapter brings a new regular customer. Answer their orders right first time during shifts to earn hearts and unlock little scenes.
  • Free practice and Challenge (ten rounds, no hints) are for extra drilling with any counters you like, ticked under Counters.
  • Keyboard: Enter = Done / Next, 1–4 = pick an answer, H = hint, R = replay audio.
