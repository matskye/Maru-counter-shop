How to play & study tips
  • 🛒 Serve: a customer orders with a number and a counter, like 「3本ください」. Put that many matching items on the counter (click, tap or drag), then press Done!
  • 🧾 Count: items are on the counter. Pick (or type) the right way to say how many there are. Watch for sound changes such as さんぼん and いっぽん.
  • 🏁 Challenge: ten rounds, no hints. Try to beat your best score.
  • Counters you miss come back more often. Open 📖 Counters for a cheat sheet, your progress, and to choose which counters to practice.
  • Keyboard: Enter = Done / Next, 1–4 = pick an answer, R = replay audio, H = hint.
